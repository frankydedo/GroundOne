# GroundOne 2026 - EDA report (dataset)

**Raw file**: `raw/GroundOne 2026 - EDA report (dataset)_ingested.md`.

## Overview
The EDA of the final GroundOne dataset (see [[Datasets for GroundOne]]):
- **Part A** covers the three sources: [[RAGTruth]] (training source, test T1), [[LLM-AggreFact]] (test T2) and [[VitaminC]] (test T3).
- **Part B** covers the dataset built from them (counts in `Project/data/processed/groundone/DATACARD.md`).

## Key findings
- **RAGTruth.**
  - Hallucinations are concentrated in data-to-text (2–4× the rate of QA or summaries) and in Llama-2/Mistral answers.
  - *Baseless* spans outnumber *Conflict* spans; *Subtle Conflict* is very rare (201).
- **Segmentation.**
  - pysbd gives 5 % fewer sentences than the earlier regex splitter (no cuts inside decimals).
  - 8.2 % of sentences have no checkable content: mostly list headings ending with ":".
- **T1 is cleaner than train.** It has a lower share of contradicted and not-mentioned sentences in every task and for every generator. Base-rate-dependent metrics (precision, F1) are not comparable between dev and T1.
- **The contradicted class is mostly "evident".** 85 % of contradicted training sentences come from *Evident Conflict* spans alone, so subtle contradictions cannot be evaluated separately.
- **Surface cues.**
  - The share of a sentence's content words found in the document orders the classes in all three sets (supported > contradicted > not mentioned). Alone it reaches **AUROC 0.74** (supported vs not) on T1, and 0.70 on VitaminC.
  - A sentence-only classifier reaches a three-way balanced accuracy of 0.66 on Data2txt, driven by Yelp details such as "100 reviews", "rating google", "wifi" and "valet garage", and is at chance on QA and Summary.
- **VitaminC.**
  - Claims are short (median 18 tokens) against one-sentence evidence (median 36).
  - 44–71 % of claims contain a digit.
  - The real test split is unbalanced (50 / 29 / 21 %).

## Consequences recorded
- Report three no-reading baselines (majority, word overlap, sentence-only n-grams), per task.
- Use base-rate-independent metrics as primary.
- Report per task and per generator group.
- Present the contradicted class as mostly evident contradictions.
