# GroundOne 2026 - EDA report

**Raw file**: `raw/GroundOne 2026 - EDA report_ingested.md` (Phase 1a exploratory data analysis, 2026-10-05). Scripts: `EDA/scripts/01_anli.py`, `02_ragtruth.py`, `03_minicheck_aggrefact.py`, `04_cross.py`. Data: `Project/data/raw/` (manifest `Project/data/manifest.json`).

## Overview
Our own analysis of the four candidate datasets for [[GroundOne]]: **[[ANLI]]** and **[[RAGTruth]]** in depth, **[[MiniCheck Synthetic Data]]** and **[[LLM-AggreFact]]** more briefly. The question it answers is *which dataset can serve which experiment* (X1–X7). The resulting data plan is synthesised in [[Datasets for GroundOne]].

Experiments the data must serve:

| ID | Experiment |
|---|---|
| X1 | Binary groundedness: training data (RQ1) |
| X2 | Binary groundedness: evaluation comparable with published results (RQ1, RQ5 plain task) |
| X3 | K decisions over one shared context: latency and parallel scoring (RQ2) |
| X4 | Three-way question: supported / contradicted / not mentioned (RQ5) |
| X5 | Rules in the prompt: strict vs lenient definition of an error (RQ5) |
| X6 | Calibration: dev for temperature, test for ECE / selective prediction (RQ3) |
| X7 | Demo realism: RAG answers to user questions over retrieved passages |

## Key Insights

### Measured (counts computed by our scripts)
- **LLM-AggreFact's RAGTruth subset is RAGTruth.** 31,711 claims (dev 15,340, test 16,371). Matched to RAGTruth sentences: 14,942 to RAGTruth *test*, 14,046 to RAGTruth *train*, 359 ambiguous (test/train), 2,364 unmatched. Its dev part comes from RAGTruth train, its test part from RAGTruth test.
- **Label agreement 98.6 %** between our span-derived binary sentence label and LLM-AggreFact's label on 29,191 matched sentences. Cross-tab (rows ours, cols AggreFact; 1 = supported): 0/0 = 2,518; 0/1 = 419; 1/0 = 1; 1/1 = 26,253. All 419 disagreements are "we say unsupported, AggreFact says supported", and are not explained by the `implicit_true` / `due_to_null` flags.
- **RAGTruth sentence classes**: 138,239 sentences. Train: 105,572 supported / 8,077 not mentioned / 4,658 contradicted. Test: 18,331 / 999 / 602. 77 % of contradiction spans come from Data2txt; in QA test only 0.5 % of sentences are contradicted.
- **ANLI premise reuse**: 10,761 unique premises, 15.73 hypotheses each on average; 98.62 % of dev and 98.88 % of test premises also occur in train. Filtering them out leaves 93,480 train pairs (24,455 C / 38,604 N / 30,421 E).
- **ANLI negation shortcut**: in train, 25 % of contradiction hypotheses contain a negation word vs 8–11 % for the other classes; in dev/test the pattern reverses (contradictions 8–9 %, supported 15–16 %). Not-mentioned claims share fewer words with the premise (median overlap 0.50 vs 0.64–0.67).
- **Lengths (Qwen2.5 tokens, no chat template)**: median document ANLI 73, MiniCheck synthetic 186, RAGTruth QA 308, RAGTruth Summary 552, LLM-AggreFact test 530. ClaimVerify and WiCE reach p90 of 3,600–4,700 tokens. RAGTruth prompts: 22 % > 1,024 tokens, 1 % > 2,048. LLM-AggreFact test documents > 2,048 tokens: 2.4 %.
- **K in RAGTruth**: median sentences per answer 9 (Data2txt), 6 (QA), 5 (Summary); 45 % of answers have K ≥ 8.
- **RAGTruth splits are clean**: no `source_id` in both train and test. 173 responses are flagged (`incorrect_refusal` 144, `truncated` 29; 17,617 `good`).
- **No overlap** between MiniCheck synthetic data (documents or claims) or ANLI premises and LLM-AggreFact.
- **MiniCheck synthetic**: C2D 7,076, D2C 7,319; supported rate 0.58 (C2D), 0.35 (D2C); document median 188 tokens (p90 276), claim median 17 tokens, 2.34 claims per document.

### Interpretations and recommendations (the report's claims)
- **No single dataset covers everything**: ANLI carries the three-way question and the rules (only balanced human three-way labels), RAGTruth carries K-decisions and in-domain training, LLM-AggreFact carries comparability.
- The 419 disagreements are attributed to "a different overlap rule or segmentation" (likely cause, not verified).
- The reversed negation pattern is attributed to ANLI's adversarial collection.
- A **2,048-token cap** keeps 99 % of RAGTruth; 1,024 would drop about a fifth (mostly Summary and Data2txt).
- With three questions per sentence, K = 15–27 per answer is realistic, the regime where Phase 0b measured a 2–6× gain.
- First proposed training mix: all 14,395 MiniCheck synthetic pairs + ~21K ANLI hard subset (premises not in dev/test) + rebalanced RAGTruth train (quality = good). Budget ≈35K examples for 0.5B (≈3–4 h), ~10K for 3B (one night).
- Phase 1 should add a claim-only baseline for ANLI and extend the leakage check to LLM-AggreFact test claims.

### Limitations (stated in the report)
- Rule-based sentence splitter; 6.1 % of RAGTruth spans cross a sentence boundary and are assigned to every sentence they touch.
- Token counts exclude the chat template (~60 extra tokens per prompt).
- Only 27 % of LLM-AggreFact RAGTruth documents match RAGTruth sources exactly (formatting differs); the overlap conclusion rests on claim matching (exact for 92 % of the subset).
- Minor internal inconsistency: a figure caption gives the MiniCheck median document length as "≈ 290 tokens", while the text gives 186–188.
