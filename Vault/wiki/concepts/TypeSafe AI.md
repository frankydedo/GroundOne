# TypeSafe AI

**TypeSafe AI** is the company behind [[Jev]]. Founded by Diogo Almeida (former OpenAI researcher and co-inventor of RLHF), they are an AI lab building machine-native intelligence infrastructure for automation.

They are the originators of the term [[System One Models]] to describe AI systems designed not for generating text, but for taking structured decisions that software can use directly. They also developed the training algorithm [[Reinforcement Learning with Calibrated Decisions]] (RLCD).

Website: https://typesafe.ai/
